## 🎯 Business Problems & Solutions

### 1. Mid-Quarter Sales Drop (February Slump)
* **Problem:** Data revealed a sharp drop in revenue during February ($97.2K) compared to January ($116.3K).
* **Solution:** Identified seasonal trend patterns and recommended targeted mid-quarter promotions and inventory adjustments to stabilize revenue streams.

### 2. High Revenue vs. Low Margin Risk
* **Problem:** Despite generating $322.91K in total revenue, the gross margin percentage averaged only 4.76%, exposing the business to high operational cost risks.
* **Solution:** Structured product-line performance views (e.g., Food & Beverages vs. Health & Beauty) to highlight high-margin categories and optimize shelf-space allocation.

### 3. Sales Team Performance Disparity
* **Problem:** Significant performance imbalance among Business Developers, with Samy driving ~$128K while Samira generated only ~$8K.
* **Solution:** Isolated individual BD performance metrics to enable targeted sales mentorship, workflow audits, and balanced lead redistribution.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics & Features
* **Financial Overview:** Tracked Revenue ($322.91K), COGS ($307.53K), and Gross Income ($15.38K).
* **Demographics & Segmentation:** Analyzed revenue across branches (Yangon, Mandalay, Naypyitaw), customer loyalty status, and gender.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Implemented time-period filtering (January, February, March) for dynamic decision-making.

---

## 💡 Strategic Insights
* **Revenue Drivers:** Food & Beverages and Electronic Accessories generated the highest gross income.
* **Customer Retention:** Revenue split between Members ($164K) and Non-Members ($159K) is nearly equal, highlighting an opportunity to expand loyalty program incentives.
